# Scene catalog: what already exists (read before making a scene)

One row per scene. A new scene must differ from **every scene in its pool on at
least three** of the columns (subject, setting, layout, vantage, light/palette)
and must not use a motif from "Overused" below as its main subject. Append a row
whenever a scene is added; do not rewrite other rows.

## Overused (do not make these the main subject again)
- **Red barn** (orchard, Christmas morning, harvest farm, pumpkin patch, tree farm).
- **Lake + cabin + dock** (golden aspen, hunter's moon, aurora cabin).
- **Lighthouse on a point** (harbor, Marblehead).
- **Subject dead centre on a knoll** (observatory, Halloween hollow, haunted manor).

## Not done yet (good directions)
Coastline cliffs or dunes, a river town from a bridge, a canyon or mesa, a
vineyard, a ski village, a train crossing a trestle, a boardwalk, a mill pond
with a waterwheel, a ferry, city brownstones in snow, a rainy street at night,
a forest trail, a wetland at sunrise, a mountain road with switchbacks, a
desert under stars, a botanical glasshouse, a ballpark, a county fair.
Vary the vantage too: high (looking down a valley), low (from the water), a
road or path leading in, a frame of near branches.
